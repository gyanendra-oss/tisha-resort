Our Story

Agra's Premier Private Event Destination

Located in the historic city of Agra, Tisha Resort & Banquet was established with a singular vision: to provide a breathtaking, flexible, and premium canvas for life's most important celebrations.

We understand that every event is unique. Unlike traditional hotels that mandate their own catering and decor, we offer a dedicated private event venue where you have the freedom to bring your own vendors. This ensures your celebration is exactly as you envisioned it, without compromises.

From the expansive Grand Lawn that can host up to 900 guests, to our intimate Poolside Venue and elegant AC Banquet Hall, every space has been designed to offer luxury, comfort, and grandeur.

The Tisha Advantage

Why Choose Us

Spacious Green Lawn

Our beautifully manicured grand lawn comfortably accommodates up to 900 guests, offering a lush, open-air setting for majestic weddings and receptions.

Poolside Venue

An exquisite poolside area perfect for intimate gatherings, Haldi, Mehendi, cocktail parties, and vibrant evening celebrations under the stars.

AC Banquet Hall

A fully air-conditioned, elegant indoor space ideal for corporate conferences, elegant dinners, and celebrations during any season, seating up to 300 guests.

Flexible Vendor Policy

We provide the stunning backdrop; you bring the vision. You have the complete freedom to bring your own caterers, decorators, and entertainment.

Ample Parking

Convenience for your guests is our priority. Tisha Resort features extensive, secure parking facilities to ensure a hassle-free arrival experience for large gatherings.

Privacy & Exclusivity

As a dedicated event venue rather than a busy public hotel, your celebration enjoys the privacy and exclusive attention it deserves without outside interruptions.
