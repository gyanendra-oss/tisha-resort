Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Tisha Resort & Banquet ("we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1 Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our website, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name — to identify you and personalize communication
  • Phone Number — to contact you regarding your inquiry
  • Email Address — for follow-up communication (if provided)
  • Event Details — event type, date, guest count, and special requirements

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your venue booking and event inquiries
  • To coordinate venue visits and event planning
  • To send you relevant updates about your booking (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and services based on user interactions

3 Data Storage

Your form submission data is securely stored via Google Sheets through Google Apps Script. This data is used exclusively by Tisha Resort management for responding to your inquiry and is not shared with any third party for marketing purposes.

4 WhatsApp Communication

When you submit an inquiry, your message may be redirected to our WhatsApp business number for faster communication. This is done via the WhatsApp Web API and your data is shared only with the Tisha Resort team. We do not store WhatsApp chat data on our website.

5 Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website uses some third-party services which may collect anonymous usage data.

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. These third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

6 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data but commit to using industry-standard practices to protect it.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to or deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of any future communications
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@tisharesort.com or call +91 92676 75757.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
